Underlying ideas: definitions to learn

CFSTR
A continuous flow stirred tank reactor. Liquid flows in and out all the time, and a stirrer keeps the contents the same everywhere.
Steady state
Nothing in the tank changes with time. The flow rate in equals the flow rate out, so the volume stays constant, and the concentrations stay constant too.
Perfectly mixed
The concentration is identical at every point in the tank. The output stream therefore has the same concentration as the tank.
Flow rate
The volume of liquid passing a point in one minute, here in cm³ per minute.
Average residence time
The average time a portion of liquid spends in the tank. In one residence time, a volume of liquid equal to the tank volume has flowed through.
