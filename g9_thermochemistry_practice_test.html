How this practice test works

  • The practice test takes about 95 minutes. The time for each section is at the top of the section. You can do it in parts.
  • Every question has a cheat sheet, up to three hints, and a worked answer that appears after you check.
  • Press Check as often as you like. A ✓ means correct. A ✗ means not yet. Read the message beside a ✗: it tells you what to look at.
  • Questions with steps show you the steps. Working scores marks in your real test, so write every step.
  • More practice gives you a new question of the same kind, with its own cheat sheet and hints. It does not change your score. Use it until you can get full marks first time without a hint.
  • Write answers to calculations as numbers. The unit is shown beside the box. Type formulae on one line, for example Ca(OH)2.
  • The Reference sheet button (top right) opens the table of ions, the molar masses, the bond enthalpies and the calorimetry formulae. Move the pointer over a dotted word to see what it means. The glossary is at the end.
  • Save progress keeps your answers in a file. Retrieve progress brings them back, so you can stop and carry on later. Save as a .pdf file opens the print window: choose Save as PDF as the printer.
  • At the end, the Feedback panel tells you which sections are secure and what to do next.
