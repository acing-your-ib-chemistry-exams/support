Glossary

Group
A vertical column of the periodic table. Elements in the same group have the same number of electrons in their outer shell, and so react in similar ways.
Period
A horizontal row of the periodic table. Elements in the same period have the same number of electron shells.
Chemical symbol
The one- or two-letter shorthand for an element, e.g. Na for sodium. First letter capital, second letter (if any) lower-case.
Precipitate
An insoluble solid formed when two solutions react together.
Limewater
A solution of calcium hydroxide, used as the standard chemical test for carbon dioxide gas — it turns cloudy/milky.
Electrolysis
Splitting a compound apart using an electric current passed through it.
Cathode / Anode
The negative electrode (cathode) and positive electrode (anode) in an electrical cell. In electrolysis, hydrogen is produced at the cathode and oxygen at the anode.
Ion
An atom, or group of atoms, that has gained or lost electrons and so carries an overall electric charge.
