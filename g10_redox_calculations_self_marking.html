How this task works

  • The main task takes about 60 minutes. The time for each section is at the top of the section. The extension and More practice are extra. You can finish it for homework.
  • Every question has a cheat sheet, up to three hints, and a worked answer that appears after you check.
  • Press Check as often as you like. A ✓ means correct. A ✗ means not yet.
  • More practice gives you a new question of the same kind, with its own cheat sheet and hints. It does not change your score. Use it until you can get full marks first time without a hint.
  • Write oxidation states with their sign: +3, -2, 0. Write answers to calculations as numbers. A small rounding difference is accepted.
  • Type equations on one line, for example Ca -> Ca2+ + 2e-. Write a charge after the symbol (2+ or 2-) and electrons as e-. State symbols are optional. The order of the terms on one side does not matter. A multiple of the half-equation is accepted.
  • Questions marked Working show you the steps. They score marks, so write them even when you are not sure of the final answer.
  • The Reference sheet button (top right) opens the oxidation state rules, the formulae for moles and the steps for half-equations. Move the pointer over a dotted word to see what it means. The glossary is at the end.
  • Save progress keeps your answers in a file. Retrieve progress brings them back, so you can stop and carry on later. Save as a .pdf file opens the print window: choose Save as PDF as the printer.
  • At the end, the Feedback panel tells you which sections are secure and what to do next.
