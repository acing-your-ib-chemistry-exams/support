Organizing the Elements of the Periodic Table

This guide walks you through the video in order, assuming no chemistry background. Most questions mark themselves — if you get one wrong, you'll get a hint and a chance to fix it before moving on.

How this works

  • Each section has its own ▶ Watch this section button — it opens the video on YouTube in a new tab, already cued a few seconds before the right moment, so you don't need to hunt for the timestamp yourself.
  • Watch in order. Each numbered section tells you what to look for before you watch that stretch.
  • Most questions have a Check answer button. Get it wrong and you'll see a hint — try again. You get a few tries before the answer is revealed, so you can correct yourself rather than just being told you're wrong.
  • If an answer does get revealed, it means the guide thinks you didn't get that bit from the video — you'll be asked to rewatch that section. Do it; guessing your way through is tracked and noted for your teacher.
  • A few questions are marked written response — these can't be auto-marked, so your teacher will read those ones directly.
  • A section turns green once everything in it has been answered (correctly, or with the answer revealed after a few tries).
  • At the end, click Generate my export to build a text block showing your answers, your score, and any rewatch flags — paste the whole thing into Canvas.
