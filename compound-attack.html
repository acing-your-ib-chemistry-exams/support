🧪 Compound Formulae Attack

Build real ionic compounds from their names. Choose the right cations and anions, fly them into the Assembly Zone with the arrow keys or WASD, balance the charges — then write the formula.

  1. A compound name appears at the top, e.g. magnesium chloride.
  2. Click the matching ion(s) to launch a copy toward the Zone.
  3. Steer with arrow keys / WASD (or the on-screen pad on touch devices).
  4. When the charges balance the Zone lights up — type the formula to score.
