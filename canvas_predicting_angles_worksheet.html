Predicting the angles: eighteen molecules and ions

Formula, structural formula and displayed formula are given. You predict the shape and bond angle at each atom.

Safety

No chemicals are used. A pencil and an eraser are all you need.

Learning objectives

You can:

  • match a formula, a structural formula and a displayed formula for the same molecule or ion;
  • find every carbon, nitrogen, oxygen, sulfur and phosphorus atom in a displayed formula;
  • predict the shape and the bond angle at each of those atoms from the chart.

Key ideas

Each box below describes one species in three ways. Ethanol, with 9 atoms, shows how they relate:

Ions have square brackets and an overall charge in the corner. A small + or − beside an atom shows the charge on that atom. In the displayed formula an oxygen atom with a − sign has one single bond only.

A displayed formula is drawn flat. Predict the real angle from the chart, not the angle you see on the page.

Underlying ideas: definitions to learn

Covalent bond
A shared pair of electrons between two atoms.
Formula
The number of atoms of each element in one molecule or ion.
Structural formula
The atoms written in the order they are joined, with groups kept together.
Displayed formula
A flat drawing of every atom and every bond.
Ion
An atom or group of atoms with an overall electric charge.
Bond angle
The angle between two bonds that meet at the same atom.
Shape
The three-dimensional arrangement of the atoms joined to a central atom.
