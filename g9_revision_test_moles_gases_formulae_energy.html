How this test works

  • Every question has a cheat sheet, up to three hints, and a worked answer that appears after you check.
  • Press Check as often as you like. A ✓ means correct. A ✗ means not yet.
  • More practice gives you a new question of the same kind, with its own cheat sheet and hints. It does not change your score.
  • Type numbers only. The unit is next to the box. A full stop or a comma is fine for decimals.
  • Type formulae with the numbers on the line, for example Ca(OH)2. Capital letters matter. Use round brackets.
  • For explanations, write full sentences. The test looks for the key ideas and shows you a model answer. Tick any idea you included in different words.
  • The Reference sheet button (top right) opens the atomic masses, the table of ions and all the formulae. Move the pointer over a dotted word to see what it means. The glossary is at the end.
  • Save progress keeps your answers in a file. Retrieve progress brings them back, so you can stop and carry on later. Save as a .pdf file opens the print window: choose Save as PDF as the printer.
  • The extension section has more challenging questions and is marked separately.
