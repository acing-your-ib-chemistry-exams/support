Curved graphs & the gradient at a point

Many experiments yield non-linear data, not a straight line — the gradient then only has meaning at a single instantaneous point. Drag the point to choose where on the curve you want the gradient, then drag the tangent's free end until the line just touches the curve there. A shaded triangle marks the rise and run along your tangent — gradient = rise ÷ run.
